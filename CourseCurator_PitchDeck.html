02. The Paradigm Shift

If you want to know how a student learns, watch what they do.

We Don't Ask. We Derive.

Course Curator AI is driven by a passive, behavioral inference engine. We never ask a student how they learn. Our Agentic AI silently observes their natural interactions during their first video session.

Behavioral Signals

  • Rewinds & Pauses: Indicates Auditory preference.
  • Panel Dwell Time: Which generated content do they stare at?
  • Quiz Speed: Active vs Reflective processing.

Instant Adaptation

  • Detects Visual, Auditory, Read/Write, or Kinesthetic styles.
  • Reorders the UI instantly to prioritize their preferred format.
  • Achieved within just 45 seconds of usage.

04. Proactive Micro-Interventions

Course Curator AI doesn't just passively observe; it actively steps in when it detects friction, just like a human tutor.

Contextual Pause Analysis

When a student pauses the video, a tiny, non-intrusive micro-prompt asks: "Taking notes ✍️, Confused 🤔, or Bored 🥱?" We instantly map their exact intent to their learning profile rather than guessing.

Answering Style Evaluation

We track quiz response times and answer-changing behaviors to classify learners as Reflective (slow, accurate) or Active (fast, trial-and-error), adjusting the pace of future questions.

Dynamic Content Simplification

If the Agentic AI detects confusion (via "Confused" pauses or failed quizzes), it autonomously regenerates the summary using "Explain Like I'm 5" (ELI5) analogies to bridge the knowledge gap.

The Agentic Study Buddy

A proactive AI that intervenes right when needed: "I noticed you struggled with the last concept. I just generated a simpler visual flowchart for that exact idea. Let's look at it."

05. Generative vs. Agentic

Generative AI creates content. Agentic AI takes action. We use both.

The Autonomous Orchestrator

Most platforms use AI as a chatbot (Generative). We use AI as an orchestrator (Agentic).

  • Autonomous Trigger: The moment a video loads, the agent fires 5 parallel prompts to Gemini. No user input required.
  • Self-Healing JSON: The agent demands strict JSON schemas. If the LLM hallucinates formatting, the agent catches it and retries autonomously.
  • Fallback Strategy: If API limits are hit, the agent gracefully degrades to rich, pre-built offline mocks—ensuring zero downtime during demos.
  • Profile Computation: The agent acts as a silent observer, calculating real-time engagement vectors to update the student's Learning Profile.

06. Future Vision & Scaling

Where Agentic AI takes Course Curator next: pushing the boundaries of autonomous education.

The "Self-Healing" Curriculum
